# Our Work research notes

## PersueKey
Source: https://www.persuekey.com/

PersueKey appears to be a shopping-discovery and coupon platform. The homepage exposes top shopping categories, trending stores, featured coupons, and featured products. It connects shoppers with store pages, discount codes, and product offers. Visible content includes coupon codes, verified labels, GET CODE/GET DEAL actions, store categories, and product price comparisons. The strongest positioning angle for a case study is a commerce discovery experience that combines searchable categories, store discovery, affiliate-style deals, coupon visibility, and product-led browsing.

Potential case-study framing: “A discovery-first commerce platform that turns scattered deals into a structured shopping journey.” Avoid publishing unverified traffic, conversion, savings, or revenue claims unless the owner provides analytics.

## GoCoupon
Source: https://www.gocoupon.io/

GoCoupon presents itself as a free Chrome extension that automatically finds and applies the highest-saving coupon code at checkout across Indian e-commerce stores. Its homepage highlights supported stores including Flipkart, Amazon, Myntra, Meesho, Ajio, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ, and FirstCry. The core flow is simple: install the extension, shop normally, and let the product test codes and apply the winner. Visible features include auto-apply coupons, affiliate cashback, privacy-first messaging, price-drop alerts, daily code verification, store coverage, deal pages, free/pro pricing, testimonials, FAQs, and blog content.

Potential case-study framing: “A one-click savings product designed to turn coupon-hunting friction into automated checkout value.” The site displays product metrics such as active users, reviews, stores supported, codes tested, and savings; these should be treated as client-provided/public claims and not repeated as independently verified outcomes without approval.

## MatchingProp
Source: https://www.matchingprop.com/

MatchingProp presents itself as a prop-trading-firm comparison directory with verified firm profiles, rule comparisons, discount coupons, reviews, futures and forex hubs, calculators, payout and integrity content, and trader-focused resources. The homepage highlights comparison filters, rankings, firm metadata, maximum allocations, platform/asset coverage, promo codes, community logs, reviews, exclusive deals, alerts, loyalty points, and risk disclosures. The strongest positioning angle is a trust-and-comparison product that turns complex prop-firm rules and promotional information into a structured decision tool for traders.

Potential case-study framing: “A verified comparison matrix that helps traders evaluate rules, payouts, platforms, and active discounts before committing to a challenge.” Avoid implying financial advice or guaranteeing trading outcomes. Keep the financial-risk disclaimer and affiliate disclosure visible on any public case-study page.
