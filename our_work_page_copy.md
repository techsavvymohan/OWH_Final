## 1. Recommended Navigation and Button Copy

Use **Our Work** as the main navigation label. It is short, immediately understandable, and more professional than “Portfolio” for a company that builds software platforms, commerce products, growth systems, and conversion-focused websites.

Use **Explore Our Work** for the homepage button. This is clearer and more inviting than a generic “View Projects.” On the Our Work page, repeat **Discuss a Similar Project** after every project card and again at the bottom of the page so visitors can move from inspiration to enquiry without searching for the next step.

It should describe the product and user experience without implying financial advice or guaranteed trading outcomes.

## 9. Important Publishing Notes

Use the public project descriptions above as positioning copy, not as proof of independently verified performance. Before publishing, confirm the correct project names, ownership relationship, permission to display each logo or screenshot, brand guidelines, live URLs, and any approved metrics. For GoCoupon and MatchingProp in particular, retain privacy, affiliate, simulated-trading, and risk-disclosure language where relevant. A strong case-study page should create desire without making unsupported financial, savings, ranking, or revenue claims.
